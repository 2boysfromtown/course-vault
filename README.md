# course-vault

**The full source, lessons and failure logs behind the way we make AI animation and video at 2BFT.** This page is the index. The files themselves live in a private repo that students of the **Claude + Magic Skills** course get access to.

This is not a pack of finished videos to resell. It is how the work is actually done: the project files, the Claude Code skills, the prompts that worked, and the long list of things that broke, so you can make your own.

👉 **Get access with the course at [2bft.in](https://2bft.in).** Buyers are invited to the private repo (read-only) after purchase.

## What's inside

Everything below runs on your own Windows PC with an NVIDIA GPU. Where a voice is needed, the lessons use open models that run locally (Chatterbox for English, AI4Bharat IndicF5 for Tamil and Hindi); paid APIs appear only as optional examples.

| # | Module | What you learn to make |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | **Your own voice, offline** | Record ~4 minutes of yourself, then speak any script in Tamil, Hindi or English in your own cloned voice, on your own GPU. Our clones scored 0.93–0.94 against our real voice. |
| 02 | **Anime in code** | A ten-step guided build from a blank script to a voiced, lip-synced, scored 1080×1920 Reel, drawn entirely in code (no AI images), then dubbed to another language. |
| 03 | **3D character films** | Draw a character with Codex, cut it into sprites, give it a local voice, and make it talk in a vertical film that renders on your GPU. A 15-second film rendered in 22 seconds for us. |
| 04 | **AI video on your GPU** | Codex draws the first frame, WAN 2.2 makes it move, locally, with no per-clip fee. Includes the real cost: ~37 GB of models and the full download-and-VRAM failure log. |
| 06 | **The Prompt Vault** | 28 image and video prompts that produced usable results for us, each with proof and the exact lines to change for your own project. |
| 07 | **Ad in a Day** | One product photo and five lines become a finished Tamil (or any-language) ad, voiced locally, loudness-checked, cut to 9:16 and 1:1. |

Every module ships a `README.md` guided lesson, a `LESSONS.md` of what broke and how we fixed it, the working `src/`, Claude Code `skills/`, and short `examples/` so you can check your result matches ours.

## FAQ

**Do I get finished videos?** No. You get the workflows, source and templates to make your own. That is the point.

**Can I use what I make commercially?** Yes, your own output is yours. You may not re-share, resell or re-upload the vault itself.

**What do I need?** A Windows PC with an NVIDIA GPU (we use an RTX 5070 Ti, 16 GB). Each module lists exactly what it was tested on.
